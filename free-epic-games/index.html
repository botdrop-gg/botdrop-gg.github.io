Questions

Are these games really free to keep?

Yes. Everything listed is a time-limited 100%-off giveaway: claim it on the store before the end time and it stays in your library for good. Free weekends and free-to-play games aren’t listed. Steam add-ons are shown separately because they need the base game.

How up to date is this list?

It refreshes itself every 3 hours from Epic’s and Steam’s own store data (US store). Those feeds aren’t officially documented and offers can vary by region, so always check the store page before claiming. End times are shown in your own time zone.

Can I get these alerts in Discord?

Yes. The free, open-source Free Games Notifier Lite posts Epic giveaways through a webhook, and the Free Games Radar bot covers Epic and Steam. Our free game alerts guide explains both.
