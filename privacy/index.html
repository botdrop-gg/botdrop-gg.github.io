Privacy note

Last updated: October 2026. BotDrop is a small shop. Here is everything this site collects, in plain words.

Visit statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views, see which pages are popular and where visitors come from (referring site, country, browser, device type) and how fast pages load. It sets no cookies, does not use local storage, does not fingerprint you, and does not track you across other websites. We only see totals, never individual visitors. See Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider Kit (kit.com) and used only to send the BotDrop newsletter. Kit may record whether emails are opened and which links are clicked, so we can see what readers find useful. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. We never sell your address; it is shared only with the service providers listed on this page. See Kit's privacy policy.

Purchases

Checkout, payment and downloads are handled by Gumroad. We receive what Gumroad shares with sellers (such as your email and what you bought) to deliver products, handle refunds and answer support requests. See Gumroad's privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Who we are

BotDrop is a small software shop operated from Ontario, Canada. Contact: hello@getbotdrop.com.

Why we use your data

Newsletter: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing. Purchases and support: to fulfil your order and handle refunds. Visit statistics: our legitimate interest in running and improving the site, using aggregate, cookieless counts.

Where it is stored

Our providers (Kit, Gumroad, Cloudflare and GitHub) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe. Purchase records: as long as needed for tax, accounting and refund purposes. Support emails: as long as needed to answer you.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing hello@getbotdrop.com. To unsubscribe, use the link in any email. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data-protection authority.

California

We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by California law.

Children

This site and our products are not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this note, we will update the date at the top.
