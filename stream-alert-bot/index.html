🔴 Stream Alert Bot

Tell your Discord the moment you upload to YouTube or go live on Twitch, once per video and once per stream, from a bot you own and host.

Stream Alert Bot is self-hosted Python source that watches YouTube channels and Twitch streamers and posts to your Discord the moment someone uploads or goes live. YouTube uploads come from YouTube's public RSS feed with no API key, and @handles are resolved automatically. Twitch go-live alerts use the official Twitch API and need a free Twitch developer app (client id and secret, about two minutes). Every video and stream is announced once, even across restarts. YouTube RSS and handle resolving were live-tested, posting into a real Discord server was tested live on Oct 16, 2026 (a YouTube upload and a Twitch go-live alert, with no duplicates on the next run), and the Docker image was built and run. Beta: the official Twitch API path is unit-tested with mocked responses but not yet live-tested with real Twitch keys, and the optional keyless Twitch mode relies on an unofficial third-party service.
